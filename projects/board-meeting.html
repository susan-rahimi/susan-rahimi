Board Meeting & Resolution Management System

Project type
Enterprise Web App Dashboard
Role
UI Designer
Timeline
22 days
Process
Client Discussions, Requirements Gathering, Documentation & UI Design

This project involved designing a centralized dashboard for the National Post Company of Iran to manage board meetings, decisions, and related administrative tasks.

The goal was to create an intuitive and efficient workspace where board members could access important information, review and respond to meeting resolutions, and stay connected with their colleagues with minimal effort.

The project took 22 days and involved several rounds of discussions with the client to understand their needs, identify key requirements, and define the system’s core functionalities. These requirements were then documented and used as the foundation for the design process.

Key Dashboard Modules

  • User Profile: Educational and Professional Information
  • Work Calendar: Calendar Management & Event Scheduling
  • Meeting Resolutions: Review, Approve, or Reject Resolutions
  • Agenda Suggestions: Submit Suggestions for Meeting Agendas
  • Resolution History: Access Previously Approved Resolutions
  • Discussion Forum: Communication and Collaboration with Colleagues
  • Board Members Directory: Access Board Members’ Professional Profiles
