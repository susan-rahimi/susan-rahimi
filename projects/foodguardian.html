Project type

Role
UI Designer
Timeline

Tools

Approach
Competitive Analysis & UI Design

FoodGuardian is a food safety management platform designed to help businesses monitor product quality, identify potential risks, and maintain food safety standards. Based on a predefined proposal and feature set, I conducted a competitive analysis to guide the UI design of the mobile application, completing the project within 20 days.

Design Scope

  • Sign Up
  • Dashboard, including:
    • Real-Time Monitoring
    • Predictive Analytics
    • Compliance Management
    • Notifications & Alerts
    • Reports & Insights
    • User Management
    • Data Integration
    • Device Management
  • Support

Information Architecture & Usability Challenges

One of the main challenges was organizing a large amount of data and multiple features without overwhelming users. The information architecture needed to make complex information easy to navigate and help users find what they needed with minimal effort.

Since the application was designed for use in factory environments, where users may need to interact with the interface while attending to other tasks, clarity and ease of navigation were particularly important. The goal was to create a straightforward structure that would help users quickly locate the right section without unnecessary cognitive effort.

Next Steps

If the project were to continue, I would focus on validating and improving the experience through the following steps:

1. Usability Testing

Conduct usability testing with target users in realistic factory environments to evaluate how easily they can navigate the application and complete their tasks.

2. Simplifying the Interface

Reduce unnecessary text and rely more on familiar icons, visual cues, and clear labels to make information easier to understand at a glance.

3. Improving Information Hierarchy

Review the information architecture and navigation based on user feedback to ensure important features and frequently used information are easy to find.

4. Enhancing Accessibility

Review text sizes, color contrast, and touch target sizes to make the interface easier to use in different working conditions.

5. Evaluating Error Prevention

Identify potential points of confusion and introduce clear feedback, confirmation messages, and helpful error states to reduce user mistakes.
